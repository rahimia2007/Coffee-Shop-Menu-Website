Our Story

Ember & Oak was born from a simple conviction: the people who grow coffee deserve as much attention as the people who drink it. Founders Maya Chen and Daniel Reyes spent two years traveling through Ethiopia, Colombia, and Indonesia before opening in Logan Square.

They came home with relationships, not contracts. We pay a 40% premium above Fair Trade minimums because quality flows from dignity. Every farm partner has a name, a face, and a story we can tell you over a cup.

Our roasting philosophy is restraint — roasting to the bean's character, light enough to taste the terroir, dark enough to drink without apology.
